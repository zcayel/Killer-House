Killer House 🎃
A multiplayer horror escape scene built for Decentraland (SDK7).

You and everyone else in the house are racing against the same house — light your ritual candles, survive what's hunting you, and find the portal before the clock or your hearts run out.

How to Play

Light candles: walk up to any glowing candle and hold the interact button (left-click on PC, the tap button on mobile) to channel it. Every player has their own set of candles,
Survive: a skeleton patrols the yard and will chase you down if it spots you. Traps are scattered through the house — some telegraph before they strike, some are always live.
Escape: light enough candles and a portal opens in the backyard. Wait it out or step through to win — but it takes a few seconds to stabilize before you can actually enter.
Race the clock: you've got a limited number of hearts and a countdown timer. Lose all your hearts, or run out of time, and it's game over.
Fastest escape times are tracked on the in-house leaderboard.

Features

Fully multiplayer ritual/candle system with shared visibility and cooperative lighting
Several distinct hazards: wall spikes, a pressure-plate dart trap, a chandelier crush, fall damage, fence impalement, and a swinging pendulum blade
A patrolling/chasing skeleton enemy
Persistent tombstones marking where players died, with hover info on cause and time of death
Dynamic darkness/atmosphere, interior dust, and ambient sound
Mobile and PC support throughout
Built With
Decentraland SDK7 (TypeScript)
Created and iterated on using Decentraland Creator Hub
